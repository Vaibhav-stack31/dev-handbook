### Connect Android device via WiFi using ADB

**When to use:** Emulator issues, or you just prefer testing on a real device wirelessly.

**Steps:**

1. On your Android device:
   - Go to **Developer Options → Wireless Debugging** → Enable it
   - Tap into it → **Pair device with pairing code**
   - Note the IP:port and the 6-digit pin shown

2. On your machine:

```bash
adb pair <ip:port shown in wireless debugging>
# Enter the 6-digit pin when prompted
```

3. Verify connection:

```bash
adb devices
# Your device should appear in the list
```

**Gotcha:** The pairing IP:port and the connection IP:port are different.
Use the pairing one only for `adb pair`. After pairing, `adb devices` confirms it.
